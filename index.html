Look closer.

Before building, observe. Before observing, question. Before questioning, doubt what appears obvious.

The world is full of things we have learned to stop noticing.

  • Connections.
  • Systems.
  • Imperfections.
  • Possibilities.

Perhaps creation begins when observation becomes impossible to ignore.

So I build—not simply to produce something, but to discover what becomes visible through the process.
